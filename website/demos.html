VISION LAB · LIVE

Emotion camera

Turn on your webcam and make a face. Every second or two, a still goes to the classifier, which returns the emotion, how positive and how intense it looks, and whether a face is in view.

Your camera stays off until you start it. Frames are sent only while it runs and are never stored.

Try the emotion camera ↗

Uses the public demo API. No login or API key required.
